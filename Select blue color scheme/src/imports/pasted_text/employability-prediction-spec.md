Design a premium, high-fidelity MVP prototype for a modern institutional technology platform called Graduate Employability Prediction System.

The platform is a decision-support system for universities and higher-education institutions. It combines academic performance, student engagement, internship/placement information, skills and project records to identify students who may need employability support early enough for an advisor to intervene.

IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE:

This is NOT a job board, NOT an LMS, NOT a generic university portal, and NOT an automatic decision-making AI.

The product helps advisors answer:

“Which students may be on a trajectory toward weak employment outcomes, why might they be at risk, and what support can we provide while there is still time to act?”

AI provides predictions, explanations and prioritization. Human advisors remain responsible for decisions and interventions.
